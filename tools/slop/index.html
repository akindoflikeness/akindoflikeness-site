About the app

What’s in the bucket?

Slop is a lightweight discovery prototype for music already hosted on the Internet Archive. Browse and search the Internet Archive’s audio collection. Open a release for its full playable track list. Audio streams directly from the Archive.

Listening queue

Click an album cover to play its first track and queue the rest. Click its title to open the release details without changing playback. Up next holds the tracks you add; they play before the remaining album tracks. Playing another album replaces only the album queue. Both queues last for this visit.

Follow the sound

“More like this” searches the Archive for releases with shared tags. These are metadata matches. Artist, track, and rights information come from the original upload. Follow Full release & rights to hear the rest.

Keep what you find

Hearts save tracks in this browser, with no account. Export a JSON backup from Saved and import it on another device. Importing adds new tracks and keeps existing saves. The file contains references, not audio. Private browsing and clearing site data can remove your local copy.

Source and licence

The interface is MIT licensed, has no build dependencies, and can be hosted as static files. The music retains its own rights: check each release before reusing it.
