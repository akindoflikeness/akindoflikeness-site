Slop · the name is the invitation

Called it slop?
Here’s the stall.

Slop is a lightweight discovery prototype for music already hosted on the Internet Archive. A small starter shelf gives you up to two sample tracks from each selected release. You can also search the wider Archive and play its audio directly.

Why Slop?

“Slop peddler.” “Noise generator.” “The antithesis of creativity.” Words used to dismiss the person making the music before anyone has to listen.

If I’m a slop peddler, why not embrace it? Here’s the stall. You can hear something, dislike it, love it, or move on. You don’t need a certificate to make it or permission to enjoy it.

Follow the sound

“More like this” matches broad tags on the starter shelf and varies the artists shown. These are simple metadata matches. Artist, track, and rights information come from the original upload. Follow Full release & rights to hear the rest.

Keep what you find

Hearts save tracks in this browser, with no account. Export a JSON backup from Saved and import it on another device. Importing adds new tracks and keeps existing saves. The file contains references, not audio. Private browsing and clearing site data can remove your local copy.

Build your own stall

The interface is MIT licensed, has no build dependencies, and can be hosted as static files. The music retains its own rights: check each release before reusing it.
