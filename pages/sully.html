Sully

A place to explore music hosted on the Internet Archive. Browse recordings, follow an artist or a sound, and listen to full releases. Save music you want to return to.

Sully provides the player and discovery tools. The recordings, artwork and credits stay connected to their original Archive pages and the people who made them. “More like this” searches for releases with matching tags from the upload metadata.

Your saved tracks

Heart a track to save it in this browser. No account is needed. Use Save in Liked tracks to back up your likes, playlists and discovery preferences, or Load to bring them to another device. Loading merges with your existing library; backups contain references and metadata, not audio.

Clearing browser data removes your local collection. Saves from the earlier hosted prototype can be exported there and imported here.

Source and music

Audio and artwork come from the Internet Archive. Each release links to its original upload and rights information. The music keeps its own licence.
