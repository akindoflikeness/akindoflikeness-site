slop

A music discovery app for the Internet Archive. Browse and search its audio collection, listen to full releases, and save what you like.

Slop provides the player and discovery tools. Audio and artwork stay on the Archive. “More like this” searches for releases with matching tags from the upload metadata.

Your saved tracks

Heart a track to save it in this browser. No account is needed. Export a backup from Saved to keep a copy or move it to another device. Importing adds tracks without removing existing saves; backups contain references, not audio.

Clearing browser data removes your local collection. Saves from the earlier hosted prototype can be exported there and imported here.

Source and music

Audio and artwork come from the Internet Archive. Each release links to its original upload and rights information. The music keeps its own licence.
