slop

Music from the Internet Archive

AppAll tools

Slop is a small discovery app for music already hosted on the Internet Archive. Pick a release, have a listen, and keep what you like.

The starter shelf has 30 releases and 59 sample tracks. You can search the wider Archive, line up your next track, and use “More like this” to find releases with matching tags. The matches come from the upload metadata; they aren’t an analysis of the sound.

Your saved tracks

Heart a track to save it in this browser. No account is needed. Export a backup from Saved to keep a copy or move it to another device; importing adds tracks without removing your existing saves. Backups contain track references, not audio.

Clearing browser data removes your local collection. If you used the earlier ChatGPT-hosted prototype, export your saves there and import them here.

A small prototype

Audio and artwork load from the Internet Archive, so availability depends on the original uploads. Each release links back to its source and rights information. The music keeps its own licence.
