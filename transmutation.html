Keys

Everything is written with sharps, never flats. D# and Eb are the same note; I picked one and stuck with it. A bare letter is major, a trailing m is natural minor, and a three-letter suffix names a mode:

—Ionian (major)1 2 3 4 5 6 7
dorDorian1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 ♭7
phrPhrygian1 ♭2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7
lydLydian1 2 3 ♯4 5 6 7
mixMixolydian1 2 3 4 5 6 ♭7
mAeolian (minor)1 2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7
locLocrian1 ♭2 ♭3 4 ♭5 ♭6 ♭7

The key names the root the loop sits on. What hangs over that root is Phrygian more often than anything else; the flat second is the sound of this pack. Some samples are not in the key or tempo the filename claims. That is the ignorance and/or impatience of the creator. Apologies, and tell me if you find one.
